## 🎨 SETUP (5 MINUTA)

### 1. Otvori Canva
- Idi na **canva.com**
- Prijavi se (ili napravi account)
- Klikni "Create new design"

### 2. Izaberi Instagram Post
- Search: "Instagram post"
- Izaberi **1080 x 1350px** (standard carousel size)
- Klikni "Create"

### 3. Dupliciraj 8 puta
- Dizajniraj prvi slide
- Klikni "File" → "Duplicate design"
- Ponovite 8 puta (trebaju ti 9 total)

5. Color: **#30D1F5** (neon cyan)

### Add Icons (Optional, harder)
1. Click "Elements"

3. Dodaj 5 ikona

6. Color change: **#30D1F5** (cyan)

1. Click "Design"
2. Add "Frame" (rectangle)
3. No fill, **stroke only**
4. Stroke color: **#30D1F5**
5. Stroke width: **4px**
6. Adjust frame size to match slide edges
